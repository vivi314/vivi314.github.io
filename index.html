Research Interests

I am a first year doctoral researcher at Karlsruhe Institute of Technology (KIT) in the group Logic of Autonomous Dynamical Systems under the supervision of Prof. André Platzer. Previously, I graduated in Mathematics at KIT, where I specialized in Differential Geometry. I am interested in bridging the gap between mathematics and logic. In particular, my research lies in the interface of Differential Dynamic Logic and Differential Geometry.
